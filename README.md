### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a senior software developer with extensive industry expertise across IoT, automotive, industrial automation, telecommunications, eGovernment, and cloud ecosystems.

- 🏢 **Current Role:** Software Developer at **[wibutler](https://dbt-platform.com/)**, developing the **[wibutler IoT platform](https://wibutler-iot.com/)** for digital building technology.
- ☁️ **Specialization:** Microsoft certified in **[Azure IoT](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-gb/solutions/iot)** – designing, implementing, and maintaining high-performance cloud & edge architectures.
- 💡 **Enterprise Experience:** Decades of experience delivering mission-critical applications, real-time analytics tools, and robust enterprise backends.
